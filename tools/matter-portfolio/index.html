Tool 05 · Legal operations

Matter Portfolio & Intake Triage

Whoever approves the legal budget does not need another list of matters. They need to know which ones will cost more than was approved before the invoice arrives, which firm is quietly billing above the agreed rates and by how much, which matters have stalled, and where a new matter should go the moment it lands. This computes each of those from a synthetic portfolio of 150 matters and three years of invoices.

Budget

Matters likely to exceed budget

Projected cost is what has been billed plus the median cost of the remaining stages, learned from this portfolio's own closed matters of the same type, discounted for the share that settle before trial. The list is what to raise with counsel this week, before the overrun is a fact.

Rates

Outside-counsel billing against the guidelines

Every invoice line is checked against the guideline rate for its timekeeper class, and its description against a vagueness test. The amount above guideline is a number that can go back to the firm with the lines attached.

Intake

Where a new matter goes

Routing is a small set of ordered rules, and the trace shows which one decided. Notices are separate: they fire on the facts whatever the routing, because the excess carrier does not care who is handling the file.

Cycle time

Stalled matters and stage durations

A matter is stalled when it has been in its current stage longer than the 90th percentile of matters of the same type that have been through that stage. The stage table is the yardstick.

Method

Assumptions on the surface

Portfolio
150 matters across six types and five entities, opened over three years, with stage histories, exposures, budgets and monthly invoices from four panel firms. Firms differ in rate discipline by design; nothing is modelled on a real firm.
Projection
Median stage cost from closed matters of the same type, with the final two stages weighted at 40% to reflect settlement. Where a stage has no history, the type's planning figure is used.
Guidelines
Partner $425, associate $285, paralegal $145. Vague descriptions are matched against a short list; a real implementation uses the billing guidelines' own list and task codes.
Routing
Eight ordered rules and five notice rules. The point is not the rules, which any department will write differently, but that the decision arrives with its reasoning and the notices it triggers.
Tests
node tools/matter-portfolio/selftest.mjs checks that invoices foot, that exactly one rule fires per intake, and that the projection and rate arithmetic hold.
Synthetic data throughout. No matter, firm, or invoice here is real. nealpathak.github.io
