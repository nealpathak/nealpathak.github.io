Load your data

Paste or choose two CSVs. They are parsed in this browser and never sent anywhere — there is no server behind this page. Column names are matched case-insensitively and unknown columns are ignored.

contract_id, counterparty, category, annual_value, cap_type, cap_value, cap_carveouts, renewal_date, owner
cap_type is AMOUNT, MULTIPLE or UNCAPPED. cap_carveouts is written the way the clause reads: INDEMNITY=UNCAPPED;DATA=3x;IP=5000000.

line, layer, attachment, limit, aggregate_limit, aggregate_eroded, retention, agg_group, captive, premium
line is GL, PROF or CYBER. Layers sharing one aggregate — a true umbrella over two lines — get the same agg_group. Set captive to Y for layers your own captive writes.

Assumptions

These describe how often a contract produces a claim and how large it is. They are a starting position for a generic mid-to-large US corporate book, not a finding. Replace them with your own loss history and the figures on the previous page become yours rather than illustrative.

Calibrate from your own losses

Paste closed and open claims as category,peril,amount, one per line, plus the number of years they cover. The model fits frequency and severity per category from what actually happened and replaces the defaults. Categories and perils must match the codes used in the register.

Changes re-run the whole model.
