Tool 07 · Contracts and risk transfer

Contract Requirements & Certificates

Every customer agreement, lease and subcontract the group signs carries an insurance clause, and the sales team signed it without reading that page. Almost nobody joins those clauses to the programme the group actually carries. This does: it finds every contract whose requirements the programme does not meet, values the revenue sitting behind them, works out the cheapest set of buy-ups that clears the most, and drafts the certificate with the gaps flagged before it goes out.

Exposure

Revenue behind requirements the programme does not meet

A contract is at risk when it requires a limit the programme does not carry or a coverage it does not have at all. Notice periods, endorsements and uncapped indemnities are listed separately: they are negotiated, not bought.

Plan

The cheapest way to close it

Each step buys the option that clears the most contract value per dollar of premium, given what has already been bought. The order matters: the first step is usually most of the answer, and the last step is usually not worth it.

Certificate

Draft from the programme, gaps flagged

A certificate should be generated from the programme and the contract, not typed from the last one. When the programme cannot satisfy the contract, the draft says so before it reaches the counterparty, which is when it is cheapest to find out.

Negotiated terms

Clauses that need a lawyer, not a broker

Uncapped indemnities and notice periods longer than the carrier will endorse are contract problems. They belong on the renewal list for the commercial team, with the counterparty and the value attached.

Method

Assumptions on the surface

Register
64 live contracts across six types and five operating companies, with insurance clauses drawn from the distribution seen in practice: most ask for the standard $1m/$2m, some ask for an umbrella the group does not carry, and a few ask for a coverage it has never bought.
Programme
One general liability, auto, employer's liability and umbrella tower per entity, with blanket additional-insured, waiver and primary-non-contributory endorsements, a group cyber policy, and 30 days' notice of cancellation. One entity carries professional liability and a larger umbrella.
Buy-ups
An illustrative rate card. Real quotes replace it; the plan logic does not change.
Revenue at risk
Annual contract value, on the view that a counterparty who audits certificates can terminate or withhold. It is an upper bound, and the point is ranking, not precision.
Tests
node tools/contract-requirements/selftest.mjs checks gap detection on hand-built contracts, that the plan clears what it claims, and that the base programme is never mutated.
