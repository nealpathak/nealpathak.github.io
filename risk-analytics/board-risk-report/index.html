Board Risk Report

Which enterprise risks sit outside stated appetite, and what does the board need to hear? A register scored for residual exposure after mitigation, measured against tolerances in dollars, and written up as a report a board can actually act on.

Related note: Risk appetite is a sentence with numbers in it

Risk register

Likelihood is a 1–5 scale. Exposure is the modeled annual loss if the risk materializes, which sets the impact band. Mitigation strength discounts inherent score to residual.

Risk appetite by category

Stance sets the qualitative posture the board has agreed. Tolerance is the aggregate annual exposure the category may carry before it requires board attention.

Methodology & model simplifications
  • Impact band is derived from modeled annual exposure against the editable dollar breakpoints, so the 1–5 impact scale stays anchored to money rather than to opinion. Inherent score is likelihood multiplied by impact band.
  • Residual score discounts the inherent score by mitigation strength — strong control halves it, partial control takes a quarter off, weak control leaves it unchanged. This is a rating convention, not a measured control-effectiveness test, and it is the softest number in the model.
  • A category breaches appetite when aggregate modeled exposure exceeds its tolerance, or when any single risk in it does. Aggregate exposure is a straight sum: it assumes risks are independent and could in principle land in the same year, which overstates aggregate loss where risks are mutually exclusive and understates it where a single event drives several at once.
  • Ranking is by residual score with velocity as the tiebreak, so two equally scored risks separate on how fast they would arrive.
  • Exposure figures are single-point estimates. There is no distribution, no correlation matrix, and no aggregation across categories to an enterprise-level figure — anyone presenting one of those to a board should build it from loss data, not from a register.
  • Commentary and recommended actions are generated from the trend, velocity, and mitigation fields by fixed rules. They are a drafting aid that gets the structure and the arithmetic right; the judgment in a real board report still has to be written by a person.
