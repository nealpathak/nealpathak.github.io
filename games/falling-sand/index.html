A cellular-automata sandbox. Every pixel follows three rules — fall, displace what's lighter, react to your neighbors — and the rest is emergent. Pour sand, flood it with water, float oil on top, then set the whole thing on fire.

How it behaves

  • Density decides everything. Sand sinks through water, water sinks through oil, oil floats to the surface. Nothing is scripted — it falls out of one comparison.
  • Fire needs fuel. It clings to wood and oil while they last, then lifts off as flame and dies into smoke. Water puts it out on contact.
  • Wood burns, stone doesn't. Build a container out of stone and it holds. Build it out of wood and it holds right up until it doesn't.
  • Keys. 1–7 pick a material, [ and ] size the brush, Space pauses. You can keep drawing while paused.
