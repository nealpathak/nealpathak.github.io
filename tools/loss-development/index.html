Tool 02 · Reserving and executive reporting

Loss Development & Aggregate Erosion

The board does not ask what the triangle looks like. It asks whether this year's losses will fit inside the aggregate the captive bought, and if not, by how much, because that is the number the renewal and the capacity decision turn on. This develops the claim listing to ultimate with the two methods every actuary uses, answers the question for each policy year and line, and, because the book is synthetic, shows how close the estimate lands to the truth.

Ultimates

By policy year

Chain-ladder develops what has been reported. Bornhuetter–Ferguson leans on the priced expectation for the part not yet developed, which is why it is selected for green years where a single large claim would otherwise be multiplied by a large factor.

Triangles

Development by age

Ages are months from policy inception. The captive's policy year runs 1 September to 31 August, so age 12 is the first 31 August after inception and the latest diagonal is the evaluation date, 31 August 2026.

Backtest

The estimate against the truth

Because the book is synthetic, the eventual cost of every claim is known. This is the one place a reserving method can be checked honestly: the table compares the selected ultimate to what the seed will actually pay.

Method

Assumptions on the surface

Factors
Age-to-age factors are volume-weighted across all available policy years unless changed above. The tail is fitted by exponential decay of the last four factors above unity and capped at 1.25; a unit tail is used when factors show no decay.
Bornhuetter–Ferguson
The a-priori expected loss is the amount the captive priced for at inception, so the method is only as good as the pricing. It is applied to years at or below the chosen age, and chain-ladder elsewhere.
Aggregate
One aggregate limit per policy year per line, set at inception. Erosion is measured against the retained ultimate, after the per-occurrence retention.
Not modelled
Reinsurance reinstatements, allocated expense treatment, and discounting. Development is on a policy-year basis; an accident-year cut of the same data is a one-line change.
Tests
node tools/loss-development/selftest.mjs checks the factor arithmetic on a hand triangle and that mature-year estimates land within ten per cent of the synthetic truth.
