Combined Ratio & Underwriting Dashboard

Loss ratio, expense ratio, combined ratio, and underwriting result by line of business across multiple accident years. Built for carrier CFOs, chief actuaries, and underwriting committees who need to see where a book is making or losing money at a glance.

Input:

Premium & Losses by Line / Year

One row per accident year × line. Loss ratio = losses incurred ÷ premium earned. Expense ratio = expenses ÷ premium written (trade basis). Combined ratio = loss + expense ratios.

Drop a CSV file here or click to upload

Columns: year, line, premium_written, premium_earned, losses_incurred, expenses_incurred

All data stays in your browser. Nothing is uploaded or stored.
