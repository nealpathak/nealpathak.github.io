Aggregate erosion to board memo

A synthetic professional liability captive: roughly 1,900 claims across four policy years, arriving from two systems that agree on almost nothing. What follows is every step from that feed to the memo a board reads — and every point where a human has to agree before anything moves.

The program, its insureds, and every figure are generated from a seed in this repository. Nothing here derives from any real book of business — not anonymized, not modelled on one. A demonstration that may be shown to one insured cannot contain another's data.
