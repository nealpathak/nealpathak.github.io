What I do

Three kinds of work, one habit

The habit is to build the thing that computes an answer nobody had before, put the assumptions on the surface, and leave a person in the loop only where a person adds something. Each of the three areas below is a place I have done that end to end.

Claims and risk analytics

Loss-run pipelines, loss development, aggregate erosion, total cost of risk, and capital adequacy for a captive or a self-insured programme. Numbers that reconcile, produced daily, that a board and an actuary can both read.

Legal operations

Matter intake, coverage and funding decisions, outside counsel and administrator oversight, contract portfolios, and platform rollouts across a group. The work of making a legal function measurable without making it slower.

AI agents and their governance

Fleets of agents that do real operational work, run on a schedule with named owners, scoped data access, human gates, kill switches and audit logs. Built to a standard an examiner would accept, sponsored so it holds.

Work

Working tools on synthetic data

Each tool runs in the browser, starts from a seed committed to the repository, and ships with a self-test. None displays a dashboard for its own sake; each computes a number or a ranking and then argues for it. Nothing derives from any employer's data.

  1. 01

    Loss-Run Pipeline

    Ingests an administrator's daily loss run, standardises it, finds the defects, reconciles to the prior run, and holds the release when a person needs to look. Drop in your own file.

    Claims data · Agent run log · Exports
  2. 02

    Loss Development & Aggregate Erosion

    Chain-ladder and Bornhuetter–Ferguson ultimates with a fitted tail, then the question the board asks: how much of each policy year's aggregate will be used, and by how much will it be exceeded.

    Reserving · Executive reporting · Backtested against the synthetic truth
  3. 03

    Capital & Cash-Flow Model

    Quarterly assets, reserves and capital over three years, two thousand times, with stochastic loss ratios and reserve deterioration. Outputs the probability of breaching the capital requirement and the capital that holds it under a tolerance.

    Capital adequacy · Monte Carlo · Scenario table
  4. 04

    Agent Control Plane

    The governance layer for a fleet of ten agents: data authorisation enforced at run time, sequenced runs, human gates, kill switches with impact analysis, control coverage, and the audit log an examiner would read. Throw a switch and see what it costs.

    AI governance · Policy engine · Operations
  5. 05

    Matter Portfolio & Intake Triage

    Which matters will exceed budget before the invoice arrives, which firm bills above the guideline and by how much, which matters have stalled, and where a new matter should go, with the rule that decided shown.

    Legal operations · Invoice analytics · Rules with a trace

About

Neal Pathak

I work in-house at the intersection of legal operations, captive insurance and risk analytics, where I lead an enterprise AI programme and build the systems described above: the claims pipeline that feeds reserving and the board, the models behind renewal and capacity decisions, the platform a group's legal function runs on, and the governance that decides what AI may do and what data it may touch.

Before that I managed litigation from pre-suit through appeal, drafted transactional documents, ran a contract portfolio and a compliance platform rollout, and started automating the parts of that work that did not need a person. The through-line is the same: find the process that runs on someone's memory, and build the thing that runs it instead.

This site is built the way I build the tools: plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, no frameworks, no build step, no dependencies. Every engine has a self-test and every dataset comes from a seed you can read. The source is on GitHub.
