Two things worth saying plainly

Most of what runs unattended here is not a model. Schema mapping and chain-ladder arithmetic are ordinary software. The model-shaped work is matching records, reading a claim against a policy form, and drafting the narrative. All three are marked human-in-the-loop, because that is where judgment is being approximated.

The controls are the product. Declared mappings, exceptions held rather than applied, figures that carry their derivation, a log of who agreed to what. Swap the model and all of it still holds.
