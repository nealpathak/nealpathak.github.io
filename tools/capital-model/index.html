Tool 03 · Capital and cash flow

Capital & Cash-Flow Model

A board has two questions before every renewal: can we keep writing at this size, and what happens if a year goes badly. This projects assets, reserves and capital quarter by quarter for three years, thousands of times, and turns the answer into the numbers a decision needs: the probability of falling below the capital requirement, the capital that holds it under your tolerance, and whether the reinsurance on offer is worth what it costs.

Adequacy

Capital against the requirement, by quarter

The band is the 5th to 95th percentile of simulated paths; the inner band is the 25th to 75th; the line is the median. The dashed line is the breach threshold. What matters is how much of the band sits below it, and when.

Scenarios

What moves the number

Each scenario changes one thing from the settings above and re-runs the same random draws, so differences are caused by the change and not by noise. The last column is the capital that would keep the breach probability under the tolerance in that scenario.

Projection

Median balance sheet by quarter

Medians of each quantity across paths. Reserves carry the run-off of the existing book plus the earned share of each new year's ultimate; new years are reserved to ultimate as they earn, which is how most captives book them.

Method

Assumptions on the surface

Opening position
Reserves are the unpaid portion of the selected ultimates from the loss development tool, run off using the book's own paid pattern. Premium and expected loss ratio come from the latest policy year's pricing.
Losses
One loss ratio per policy year, lognormal around the expected ratio with the chosen coefficient of variation. Reserve deterioration on the opening book is a single lognormal draw with an 8% coefficient of variation, on top of any deterministic shock.
Required capital
35% of trailing four-quarter net written premium plus 15% of carried reserves. This is a disclosed stand-in for a regulator's or rating agency's formula; swap the factors and the rest holds.
Aggregate
When purchased, each year's retained ultimate is capped at the attachment multiple of expected loss and the cover costs the stated share of premium each quarter.
Not modelled
Fronting fees as a separate line, letters of credit, tax, discounting of reserves, and correlation between years. Each is a few lines and none changes the shape of the answer.
Tests
node tools/capital-model/selftest.mjs checks the accounting identity every quarter, a closed-form deterministic case, monotonicity in capital, and that the solved capital actually meets the tolerance.
Synthetic data throughout. Nothing here derives from any real insurer, insured, or claimant. nealpathak.github.io
