Methodology & model simplifications
  • Policies are written uniformly: 1/12 of annual written premium incepts each month, with the full annual premium collected at inception. Premium earns pro rata over the 12 months from inception.
  • The book starts cold at month 0, so year-1 figures reflect ramp-up of earned premium and in-force exposure.
  • Expenses are paid as premium is earned (a simplification; in practice acquisition costs are paid up front).
  • The loss ratio is deterministic and applied to earned premium by accident month — no stochastic frequency/severity, no trend, no reserve development beyond the payout curve.
  • Losses pay out on an industry-shaped MPL development curve stretched or compressed to the selected tail length, not company-specific triangles. Reserves shown are cumulative incurred minus cumulative paid (an IBNR proxy, not a reserving exercise).
  • Collected premium sits in cash at the uninvested-cash yield until the deployment lag elapses, then moves into the portfolio at the portfolio yield. Claims and expenses are paid from the portfolio first, then cash. Investment income compounds within investable assets.
  • Losses still unpaid at month 120 remain in reserves; the simulation window ends before the tail fully runs off.
  • No taxes, reinsurance, commissions structure, or capital charges are modeled.
  • This is an educational simulation instrument, not a pricing, reserving, or investment model.
